## Notes

- Camera jitter (`cameras: jitter`) perturbs the fixed top and front cameras per seed from a separate random stream, so all other layout quantities are unchanged. In `realistic` sensing `TwinMujoco` hand-eye calibrates the top camera of every episode from its own layout at construction (`calibrate_layout`, event `camera_calibrated`), so the skills see the jittered camera through that per-episode calibration; the intrinsics (focal) are taken as known. Spot check on six jittered train seeds: calibration converged with 40 card poses, 0.9-2.1 mm and 0.08-0.25 deg camera error, about 3 s each. `truth` sensing reads no camera pose. The bottom-view threshold profile and detector were tuned on the fixed camera (`camera_profile` `mujoco-close-top-1.35`, still recorded as such); a jittered suite needs its own expert check before collection.
- The real-layout prior (`config/sim/prior_grasp.json`, 35 real first frames) replaces the uniform positions: bricks start from real brick positions and the bowls from one essentially fixed real arrangement (pink bowl far out), each jittered a few cm (see the variants table). The expert has only been validated on uniform layouts (bench16, brick reach at most 0.25 m), so a prior suite also needs an expert check.
